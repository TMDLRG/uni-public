The seventh phase — the lab view — is planned here, and its status line says the acceptance bar was not met, with two of its clauses failing. Read that first.

The phase designs a scene as a pure function of state, and the refusals that go with rendering it. A node with no truth class, or no receipt — the file recording what was run — must render as fog. Fog is the honest depiction of an unbacked assertion rather than an error. Liveness may be shown only from a real probe. And nothing may depict awareness, experience or life.

The section headed as the one thing this phase must not get wrong states the reasoning. A picture persuades faster than it can be checked, so a scene that looks alive will be believed to be alive whatever the caption says. The bar is therefore not the absence of a misleading label. It is that a simulated fixture and an observed one are distinguishable in a screenshot with no text read.

The plan also says the author's own eye is not enough for that, and names a human-flow review to do it instead.
