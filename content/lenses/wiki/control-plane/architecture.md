This is the main architecture document, and its first line tells you how to read it: part design, part built, and it moves no confidence level by itself. Some parts described here exist and are tested; the lab view does not exist.

It describes the body that runs the science and authors every verdict. It also explains what it is not: not the witness that only projects, not the door that governs a threshold, not the display that shows and carries, and not a dashboard — because dashboards report and this body decides.

Between those, it carries what a diagram cannot: contracts, refusals, acceptance criteria, and the rules that must not break. What each field means. What every interface may and may not do. What a room requires before it opens. What a picture is allowed to imply, which is the part worth reading even if you skip the rest — no material, light or room here may depict awareness, experience or life.

This is the architecture document for the body that runs the science, and its status line is doing work: part design, part built, moving no confidence level. It records which parts landed in which phase, and states plainly that only the lab view remains unbuilt. No verdict has been authored by this body about any real scientific claim: the vocabulary exists and refuses correctly, and it has adjudicated nothing.

It opens by placing itself. A model kept elsewhere is the record; views projected from it are pictures; this file carries what neither can hold — contracts, failure modes, acceptance criteria, and the rules that must not break. Where a view and the model disagree, the model wins.

The purpose section says what this body is and, at more length, what it is not. Not the witness, which may never act or author. Not the door that governs a threshold. Not the display, which authors nothing. Not a dashboard. The non-goals include holding a stream key, typing go-live, and rendering awareness, experience or life in any form.

The problem statement names verified gaps. Most steps of the evidence path were done by hand with no home. Gate status drifted because nothing owned it — one gate was recorded two contradictory ways in two places. And there was nowhere to stand: no surface was a lab you could work in across projects.

Sections describe each of the four bodies with its contract table, its law and its gates, including one law inherited word for word, learned from two recorded incidents. The section on the projecting body records a live audit rather than a stale document. Its law is set out: it shows only direct signals with a record of where each came from, and never summarises, scores, ranks or authors. Three consequences follow for this design.

Two limitations ride with what is built, both stated rather than footnoted. A local anchor cannot outrank a local writer, so a tamperer who owns the storage directory is not caught — and a test performs that attack and asserts it succeeds. The ability to record its own changes exists without having been used.

The middle is specification. A domain model names every entity and field, carrying truth class and evidence class on every renderable and every claim. Interfaces make every change an explicit verb, let no read actuate anything, and make a refusal name the missing precondition rather than a generic error. State machines cover a gate, a run and a room.

The section on the lab view carries the render contract — the part to read even if you read nothing else. A viewer must be able to tell how sure a thing is from a still picture with no text. So each truth class has its own material: solid and lit for the observed, visibly rebuilt for a reconstruction, translucent for a calculation, visibly staged for something simulated, and fog for the unknown. Two rules follow. Nothing about motion or light may imply liveness, which comes only from a real probe. And passing a gate renders the named behaviour and nothing more, because no material, light or room here may depict awareness, experience or life. Refusal is the feature, and an unlicensed action is absent rather than greyed.

Storage, where evidence came from, authorisation, failure modes, observability and a build sequence follow, with a governance section giving three views per material decision, two adversarial.

The final section is an honest state: what is running, what is deliberately down, what is not located, and a closing line saying this document passed no gates.
