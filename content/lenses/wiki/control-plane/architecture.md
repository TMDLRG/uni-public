This is the architecture document for the body that runs the science, and its status line is doing work: part design, part built, and it moves no confidence level. It records which parts landed in which phase, states plainly that only the lab view remains unbuilt, and says no verdict has been authored by this body about any real scientific claim — the vocabulary exists and refuses correctly, and it has adjudicated nothing.

It opens by placing itself. A model kept elsewhere is the record; views projected from it are pictures; this file carries what neither can hold — contracts, invariants, failure modes and acceptance criteria. Where a view and the model disagree, the model wins.

The purpose section says what this body is and, at more length, what it is not: not the witness that may never act or author, not the door that governs a threshold, not the display that authors nothing, and not a dashboard. The non-goals include holding a stream key, typing go-live, and rendering awareness, experience or life in any form.

The problem statement names verified gaps. Most steps of the evidence path were done by hand with no home. Gate status drifted because nothing owned it — one gate was recorded two contradictory ways in two places. And there was nowhere to stand: no surface was a lab you could work in across projects.

Sections describe each of the four bodies with its contract table, its law and its gates, including one law inherited word for word because it was learned from two recorded incidents. The section on the projecting body records a live audit rather than repeating a stale document, sets out its law — it shows only direct signals with provenance and never summarises, scores, ranks or authors — and draws three consequences for this design.

Two limitations ride with what is built, both stated rather than footnoted: a local anchor cannot outrank a local writer, so a tamperer who owns the storage directory is not caught, and a test performs that attack and asserts it succeeds; and the ability to record its own changes exists without having been used.

The middle is specification: a domain model naming every entity and field, with truth class and evidence class carried on every renderable and every claim; interfaces where all mutations are explicit verbs and no read actuates anything, and refusals name the missing precondition rather than a generic error; and state machines for a gate, a run and a room.

The section on the lab view carries the render contract, and it is the part to read even if you read nothing else. A viewer must be able to read epistemic status from a still picture with no text, so each truth class has its own material — solid and lit for the observed, visibly rebuilt for a reconstruction, translucent for a calculation, visibly staged for something simulated, and fog for the unknown. Two rules follow: nothing about motion or light may imply liveness, which comes only from a real probe; and passing a gate renders the named behaviour and nothing more, because no material, light or room here may depict awareness, experience or life. Refusal is the feature, and an unlicensed action is absent rather than greyed.

Storage, provenance, authorisation, failure modes, observability and a build sequence follow, with a governance section giving three perspectives per material decision, two of them adversarial.

The final section is an honest state: what is running, what is deliberately down, what is not located, and a closing line saying this document passed no gates.
