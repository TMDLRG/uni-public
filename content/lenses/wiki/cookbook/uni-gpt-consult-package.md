This page is a small piece of working apparatus, and reading it as anything more would be a mistake. It is the ready-to-run package for a design consultation: a context block to paste first, then a set of questions ranked by how much each answer would unblock. It records that it was prepared and queued because the browser connection needed to run the consultation was not available at the time.

The context block is worth noticing on its own, because it is the programme describing itself to an outside adviser. It states the honest position — a developmental simulation with about two of eleven-plus rungs earned — and lists the standing fences it expects to be kept: no claims of general intelligence, awareness, human-level ability or created life; a reading result treated as a counting baseline and nothing more; synthetic work never inflated into capability; no claim raised above its evidence class; and negatives treated as first-class content. It then summarises which rungs are largely earned, which carry recorded negatives, which are parked, and which are not built and hard-fenced.

The questions themselves map the programme's open problems. How should a parked frontier be worded so that it stays a scoped bound rather than becoming a claim that a search is exhausted or a threshold achieved? Of several primitives missing from the substrate, which single one should be ported first, frozen at the version that passed its gate, to close the gap between a coupling rule signed in principle and one that is literally true? What is the smallest structurally distinct change that would move a metabolism gate which is open and diagnosed as a starvation of information-seeking, and what paired experiment would isolate it one cure at a time? What is the smallest falsifiable target for a rung that has no gate, complete with a tuned baseline, a discriminator that must collapse the gain, and a genuine computed ablation? What smallest downstream gate would make an offline replay effect real without ever needing the word for it? Which structurally distinct design should accrue next on the motor frontier? And what published wording keeps the awareness question falsifiable rather than answered?

The answer slots are left empty, which is what makes this a record of a moment rather than a result. A closing note says what to do afterwards: fold any sign-offs or corrections back into the plans, calibrating wording downward only, record any captured sign-off, and update the progress file.

The page also fences itself. Protected mathematics is never to be pasted, the framing stays at textbook level, and the consultation is a private design input whose output is not a publishing surface.
