This document is the cookbook in outline. It is the single buildable plan for a developmental arc, running from a first cell division through cellular upkeep, metabolism, an organ model, affect, movement, perception, language and a self-model. From there it goes on to reasoning, wisdom, offline replay and creativity. The unifying claim is modest and structural — one engine, reused at many scales, with only the world and the observation model swapped. The honest position is printed at the top and repeated at the bottom: about two of eleven-plus rungs earned, and the whole thing a developmental simulation.

It opens with the kitchen rules, set out as a table of governance patterns that bind every recipe below and are explicitly not capability claims. Every claim carries an evidence class and a falsifier, the result that would sink it, in a ledger added to and never edited. A bar is registered before the build, and a set kept back is touched once. A claim of reproduction is derived from several seeds rather than written in. Synthetic construction is diagnostic, never capability, and a bound is owed only after several structurally distinct negatives. A capability claim needs a tuned baseline and a check that collapses the gain. A passing test does not satisfy a criterion asking for an observation, and a linter downgrades an overclaim. World, body and mind stay separate, learning is counting with a guard against gradients, and numeric precision is stated honestly. What came from above is not counted as fresh evidence. Negatives are published as the pitch, a panel signs before a merge, and one change is made at a time. A long run is written down as it goes, because silence is not success. A list of standing limits follows, and then the four status words.

A shared pantry table names the engines every recipe draws on, each labelled at its real status. Then the ladder itself is walked rung by rung, and each rung is given the same shape: ingredients, numbered method, an exact gate, an operable falsifier, any recorded negatives inline, and one honest status word. The lower rungs carry passes with their limits and losses printed beside them. The upper rungs are parked or not built, and say so.

A separate section covers the substrate the whole thing runs on, marked as engineering evidence rather than science, with its own negatives carried first-class and its central coupling gap left open. It also records that an honesty audit calibrated several headline claims downward, and instructs the reader to carry the calibrated figures rather than the inflated ones.

The most distinctive section is the last. A set of open questions is listed, ordered by how much each answer would reduce uncertainty about whether a sealed gate could even be defined, and each now carries the answer it received. One refused a phrase and supplied scoped wording instead. One chose which missing primitive to port first. Three proposed first gates for rungs that had none. One proposed the next design on the motor frontier. One installed a framing guardrail for the top of the ladder. Every answer is folded in as designed and not run, and the plan states after each that the rung's status is unchanged.

The provenance note closes it: every figure, verdict, class and falsifier traces to a row in the ledger, no step is labelled above its evidence, no negative is hidden, and any step may be falsified.
