This document is the cookbook in outline: the single buildable plan for a developmental arc running from a first cell division through cellular upkeep, metabolism, an organ model, affect, movement, perception, language and a self-model, and then on to reasoning, wisdom, offline replay and creativity. The unifying claim is modest and structural — one engine, reused at many scales, with only the world and the observation model swapped. The honest position is printed at the top and repeated at the bottom: about two of eleven-plus rungs earned, and the whole thing a developmental simulation.

It opens with the kitchen rules, set out as a table of governance patterns that bind every recipe below and are explicitly not capability claims. They cover evidence classes and falsifiers in an append-only record; registering a bar before building and touching a held set once; deriving a claim of reproduction from several seeds rather than writing it in; treating synthetic construction as diagnostic rather than capability; requiring several structurally distinct negatives before a bound; resting only on a working result or a scoped exhausted envelope; demanding a tuned baseline and a check that collapses the gain; a staged evidence contract with a linter that downgrades overclaims; an ordering in which a passing test does not satisfy a criterion asking for an observation; honesty about numeric precision; separation of world, body and mind; learning by counting with a guard against gradients; publishing negatives as the pitch; a review panel that must sign before a merge; one change at a time; a rule against treating what came from above as fresh evidence; and a durable runner where silence is not success. A list of standing fences follows, and then the four fence words.

A shared pantry table names the engines every recipe draws on, each carded at its real status. Then the ladder itself is walked rung by rung, and each rung is given the same shape: ingredients, numbered method, an exact gate, an operable falsifier, any recorded negatives inline, and one honest fence. The lower rungs carry passes with their limits and losses printed beside them. The upper rungs are parked or not built, and say so.

A separate section covers the substrate the whole thing runs on, marked as engineering evidence rather than science, with its own negatives carried first-class and its central coupling gap left open. It also records that an honesty audit calibrated several headline claims downward, and instructs the reader to carry the calibrated figures rather than the inflated ones.

The most distinctive section is the last. A set of open questions is listed, ordered by how much each answer would reduce uncertainty about whether a sealed gate could even be defined, and each now carries the answer it received. One refused a phrase and supplied scoped wording instead. One chose which missing primitive to port first. Three proposed first gates for rungs that had none. One proposed the next design on the motor frontier. One installed a framing guardrail for the top of the ladder. Every answer is folded in as designed and not run, and the plan states after each that the rung's status is unchanged.

The provenance note closes it: every figure, verdict, class and falsifier traces to a row in the ledger, no step is carded above its evidence, no negative is hidden, and any step may be falsified.
