It builds a sealed report card of maturation gates, each of which has to survive a check designed to break it. Every gate passes. What that card asserts is functional self-awareness and nothing above it. Feeling and experience are disclaimed outright. This chapter is about a system that models itself, and its whole discipline is the gap between two readings of that phrase. What is built is a held report card of maturation gates, covering things like immersion, growth, a self-model, metacognition, reflection on language, consolidation, a reflective reader, vocabulary growth while running, reflection modulated by affect, and a compositional reader. The card is sealed, scored once, and every gate passes. What that pass asserts is functional self-awareness. What it does not assert is experience, and the chapter states that in its first sentence and again at its last.

The method is mostly a set of ways to stop a gate from being cheap. Every gate carrying a claim about self-modelling or metacognition must register in advance a check under which its effect has to collapse: shuffled labels, swapped markers, or the mechanism zeroed out. No gate is credited if that check fails to collapse it. Every self-model task must also be shown not to be passable by a trivial shortcut such as recency, frequency, or surface matching; a task a dumb rule can pass never tested self-modelling in the first place. Reflection driven by affect is wired through the same modulator used at an earlier rung, keeping that rung's limit intact: affect is modelled, never felt. The card is then run once behind a seal, and the verdict is not re-scored.

The falsifier, the result that would show the claim wrong, is stated item by item. A gate failing on re-run, an effect that collapses under a check it was supposed to survive, or a task shown passable by a shortcut. And then comes the sentence the chapter marks as load-bearing: no falsifier is offered for experience, because experience is disclaimed rather than tested. The absence is deliberate.

The recorded negatives keep two artefacts apart, which is the subtlest part of the page. On the reader side, self-modelling was held negative, along with selective reading, comprehension above retrieval, and writing treated as action. The passing report card comes from a different artefact and does not overturn any of that. The rule is that the reader-side negative travels with the card and is cited every time the card is cited. The second recorded negative is simply that experience is not in the pass at all, and that reading the card as awareness is itself the violation the disclaimer exists to catch.

The closing section lists the licensed forms of speech, which is unusual and worth noticing. The permitted statements are that a specified proxy test was passed, that the awareness question remains open, and that this remains a developmental simulation. Everything stronger is named and forbidden. No signed design attaches to this rung, so nothing lifts it.
