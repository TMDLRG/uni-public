This is a dated operations sheet with a superseded banner at the top. It has been replaced by a later version, and it is kept for the audit trail rather than for use. That framing matters: its own section of measured state is stale wherever it disagrees with the newer sheet, and the document says so itself.

Its purpose was recovery. After a full restart, with no chat history anywhere, the owner opens one page and puts five agents back on the same shared footing. Four sections do that.

The first is the shared discipline. Every agent runs the same observe, orient, decide, act loop the colony runs. Observe through gates rather than through the mere existence of a running process. Orient by closing the gap between what was measured and what the documents say. Decide the one next act, and act as code, document and gate together. One change at a time. A vocabulary fence — a list of words a claim may not use — is named as the thing that keeps claims honest. Verdicts are words rather than percentages, and a passing gate is said to show a named behaviour and never experience or life.

The second is the roster: who works on which machine, what each one owns, and what each must never touch. It names a shared, read-only seam where one track sets a verdict and the others may only read, cross-examine, advise within, or publish it. One of the five roles did not exist yet; this sheet was what brought it into being.

The third is the measured state on the day. The colony was up at the level of running processes, but running a mind image weeks older than the code. So a layer that should have been flying a camera was simply absent from the running build, and one claim about it is marked as not verified. The gate ladder is given as a count of passes, partials and waiting rows, with the honest note that every waiting runner was still a stub that raises rather than runs. A single redeploy is named as the change that would unblock two other stalled pieces, and it is fenced behind the owner's go-ahead and an adversarial review.

The fourth is the launch procedure, one numbered step per agent, each pointing at its own prompt file and noting where that prompt has since drifted from reality.

The closing line repeats the fence: every gate shows behaviour, and none of it is evidence of experience, awareness or life.
