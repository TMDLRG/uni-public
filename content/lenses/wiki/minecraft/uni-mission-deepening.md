This is a research synthesis rather than a plan or a report. It records how it was produced: a multi-agent workflow that first grounded the current architecture, then researched several areas against both the literature and the codebase, then adversarially stress-tested each for violations of the project's invariants and honesty rules, then combined the results, and finally refined them through consult cycles.

The mission statement is ambitious and its constraints arrive in the same sentence. The aim is a single body that perceives, learns and acts to minimise a named quantity, deepened into a coherent whole with a lateralised layer above a shared bank of factors, a segmented layer below, and internal organs with cycles within. Every layer must be additive and gated behind an opt-in inherited organ so that the standard configuration behaves identically, and every new term must reduce to a recognised quantity of the theory rather than an invented reward, with a list of techniques that are ruled out entirely.

The immediate scientific target is far narrower and concrete: break a measured plateau where one agent hoards many copies of a single tool and exploration flattens. The cure that the adversarial review converged on is named, and so is a discipline about it, which is that the diagnosis must be proved before the cure is applied, distinguishing two rival explanations.

The architecture section then describes each proposed layer with the mechanism it would use, and is careful about what stays fixed. The decision core is unchanged, and every new term threads into the real deep decider first with the shallower path as a mirror. The layer above uses two parents with different granularity over the same children, and the document is precise that one of them broadens by having a wider prior rather than by flipping a sign. The layer below generalises a servo into segments whose stiffness varies, injecting variability through the existing information term and never as a value bonus. The internal organs introduce the one genuinely new object, a set of transitions that empty and fill, with a preference peaked at a comfortable level rather than at a maximum. The glands section restricts an attenuation to a whitelist and requires one rhythm to be conditional on a real sensed signal rather than assumed.

A roadmap follows with each phase additive, gated and paired with a falsifiable test, then a list of preserved invariants, then a section of falsification signals defining when the map must be updated.

One section deals with awareness and life measures directly, and fences them: they are operational and falsifiable, necessary but not sufficient, and carry no evidential weight for experience on their own.

The final sections record open questions taken to an outside consult, an execution status, and the consult's rulings. Those rulings are the sharpest writing in the document. One requires a specific invariance test in which two identical cloned actions must produce identical outputs, and in which attaching a cost to one of them must change nothing, so that the decision can only depend on predicted outcomes rather than on the identity of an action. Another sets guardrails so that a context-dependent preference cannot become a disguised reward, requiring it to be action-independent and fixed before any policy is evaluated. A third changes the earlier proposal outright, preferring a strong prior that evidence may refine over freezing learning, and reserving freezing only for something declared to be hard physiology.
