This is a deep pre-restart handover for one track of work. It opens with a post-restart audit recording everything revalidated, points at another document for the wider cross-agent picture, and says this one is the detailed state behind it. It also notes which uncommitted files belong to a different track and should be left alone.

A revalidation snapshot lists the suites and offline checks with their results.

The arc of the session is then told in four numbered steps, each with a receipt. Two registered tests both refuted the same thing, the second designed to remove a confound the first had left open. Together they show a flat fixed preference shape is maladaptive: it dies in half its worlds with food available, and is more scattered than a hoarding foil. The document is careful to say the second test resolves the first's open question, so the deaths are intrinsic to the shape rather than an artifact.

That measured death then licenses an owner correction, quoted as binding: the model had been oversimplified, and fitting into a game body is not a licence to reduce depth. A full-depth design followed, reviewed by a large workflow and signed with changes, and the owner chose the deepest first rung.

The engine for that rung is recorded as built and checked, entirely behind an opt-in organ so the default stays byte-identical, with a chain of commits. Each file is listed with what it does, including a new body component holding four stores advanced by elapsed time with attribution to whichever subsystem acted, one of them running on a faster clock than the others, and a rule for when the body counts as dead. Other entries cover the new factors, the interior-peak preference shape, an indexing fix, and a path in the runtime that keeps the two step paths identical.

The offline dynamics are reported with numbers: the body survives where the flat shape died, holds an interior reserve rather than sitting pinned at full, paces work against rest, and shows one clean decoupling between subsystems while another pair remains honestly correlated. That honesty about which decoupling is clean and which is not is the useful part.

One entry records a fix that came from an offline pre-check: a transfer between two stores had pinned one of them full, which made the treatment indistinguishable from the foil it was meant to beat. The transfer was changed and the deferred part is named.

An invariants section states what never regressed and which fences were respected. The next steps are stated as not yet started, needing the owner's go-ahead for the live run, and one instruction stands out: the discriminator must be behavioural rather than survival alone, because the offline check showed both arms can survive.

A section of honest ceilings names what remains simplified, followed by a claim fence saying every value is a model variable and never a felt state.

The note ends with an open issue from a sibling track: a camera was found flying on its own because the component meant to drive it was not running, which is diagnosed, cleaned up, and given a restore procedure together with a framing rule about how that role must and must not be described.
