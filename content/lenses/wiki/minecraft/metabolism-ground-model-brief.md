This is a design brief with a correction banner, and the banner is the reason to read it. One of the brief's own conclusions is marked wrong and kept deliberately, so that both the error and its refutation stay in the evidence chain rather than being quietly deleted.

The refuted conclusion was that a strong prior for the new transitions would be seeded automatically, requiring no new code. It would not be, because one function normalises a column to sum to one before another adds a constant, so any magnitude you tried to build into the shape is wiped and only a mild prior survives. A dedicated seam was therefore required, and the banner names where it lives in the authoritative specification. The banner is careful about scope: the claims about the shape and direction of the transitions remain correct, and only the sufficiency of the automatic seed is withdrawn.

The brief itself is explicitly design only: nothing is edited and nothing deploys. It describes itself as the structural map that a later typed difference and an adversarial review build on.

A binding limit on what may be claimed comes first, and it is unusually direct. The internal quantities here are categorical hidden factors over discrete bins with learned tensors and a preference vector. They are named as necessary but not sufficient substrate with no evidential weight for awareness or for hunger as something felt. The words used for them name mathematical objects and never an experience, and no value in the organ may be surfaced as felt.

The first substantive section draws the pipeline a new organ must enter as a funnel, listing the four places every previous organ added itself, with the most recent one named as the pattern to copy. It notes that a component walks the active channels in declared order and feeds them positionally, so declared order is load-bearing.

The second section explains why the default configuration is untouched. The new organ is absent from the default plan, and channels are filtered by whether their organ is in the plan. So nothing about the default develops, compiles or seeds differently. It then names two genuinely new things this organ introduces, and how each preserves that property. One note says the single most sensitive edit in the whole design is extending one allow-list. Another says any new random draw must be appended last, or every existing lineage's reproducibility breaks.

The third section proposes the two new self-sensing factors, and explains why they need a weak diagonal prior rather than a flat one. A factor of that shape with a flat prior cannot be identified at all, and would sit stuck. It also flags an ordering hazard plainly: existing code takes the last few channels by position and assumes they are a particular block, so appending new channels after them would break that assumption for any configuration carrying both. The resolution offered is that the two organs do not co-occur in the registered work, so the hazard is dormant, and it is recorded here so a future combiner does not trip it.

The fourth section explains why today's transitions are wrong for a store that must drain and refill, and introduces an optional field whose absence reproduces exactly today's behaviour, so existing factors are unchanged by construction. Two additive edits are proposed and shown.

The brief closes with an edit list as a table, one row per file, each naming the change, its risk to unchanged behaviour and the mitigation. Then an audit against the project's mathematical limits, and the gate required before any code.
