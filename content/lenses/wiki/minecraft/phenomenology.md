This document maps a list of human phenomena onto mechanisms in an active-inference engine, and records where each one stops.

Its stance is set out first, and it matters more than any row. The team writes as the people trying to falsify rather than to flatter. For each phenomenon they build the strongest mechanism the mathematics supports, state a prediction it makes, test it, and record the result together with a limit on what it shows. Where the mathematics runs out, they say so and label it.

The list then runs through pain, attention, hormones, emotion, two senses of self, self-modelling, reasoning, growth, access-style consciousness, out-of-body experience, death and near-death reports, and bodily control. Each row names the mechanism, the prediction, the test, the result and the limit on what it shows.

The fences are the point. The document states plainly that it does not claim a colony member feels anything, is conscious or sentient, or that a saved model is a surviving self. It is data.

This is a tally rather than an argument, and it is organised so that every claim arrives attached to the place it stops.

The stance is declared first. The authors describe themselves as the team trying to falsify rather than to flatter. For each phenomenon they build the strongest mechanism the mathematics supports, state an observable prediction, test it, and record both the result and the limit on what it shows. Where the mathematics runs out they say so and give it a class of its own. They state that they do not assert that agents feel pain, have emotions, or are conscious, and that what an agent reports is it describing its computed state rather than evidence of experience.

Three evidence classes are declared. One for standard, well-grounded material. One for engineering or frontier proposals that are implementable and testable for adequacy but not claimed as biology. And one for the unproven or metaphysical, where the mechanism and the report are modelled and the metaphysics is not.

A covenant follows, listing the properties an automated checker enforces. There is a bound that must hold, the distinction between two ways of combining logarithms and averages, and the fact that a joint belief is never built. Then purity, what may cross the boundary, the absence of reward, and agreement with an independent implementation.

The tally then runs row by row. Pain is treated as a reallocation of precision together with an inferred bodily cause, with the prediction that a taught agent selects withdrawal. Attention is dynamic precision. Hormones are slow modulation of precision and learning rate. Emotion is a read-out over posteriors rather than a stored variable, and its sharp prediction is that removing the ability to act under threat shifts the dominant label from fear toward anger. Two senses of self are mapped onto the two timescales of the hierarchy. A self-model is an internal factor. Reasoning is deeper planning, with the stated limit that this is recursive evaluation and not understanding. Growth is structure learning under a cost. Access-style consciousness is broadcast, report and metacognition, and carries the strictest limit in the document. Out-of-body experience is a shift in a self-location posterior under skewed precision, fenced with the plain sentence that the self did not leave the body. Death is exit from viability and a shutdown of the loop, fenced to the computational end with no claim of anything surviving. Near-death reports are treated as narrative clustering, which explains why reports cluster rather than what was experienced. And bodily control is continuous predictive coding.

A closing section gathers what is not claimed. Not that any agent feels pain, fear, grief or peace. Not that any agent is conscious or sentient. Not that an out-of-body posterior means a self left a body, or that clustering says anything about an afterlife. And not that a saved model is a surviving self, which the page calls data.

The last line makes the tally's own case: every pass is a real test in the suite, and every place labelled unproven is a place the authors deliberately stop.
