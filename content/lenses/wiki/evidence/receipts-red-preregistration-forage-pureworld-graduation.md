This is a plan for a test, written before the test. It asks whether a trained system can keep itself going in a harder world where nothing has been made easy for it, while an untrained twin, given the same world, does not. That contrast is the whole point: without the untrained twin, surviving on its own would say nothing about the training. The verdict is withheld. The page ends with a short list of things this test would not settle, including anything about awareness or experience, and the authors put that limit in writing themselves.

<!--CLEAR-->
A pre-registration, with the verdict withheld. It describes a run that had not yet happened when the page was written.

The background is a gentler setting that had reduced the pressure the bodies were under while they learned to feed themselves. This test takes that help away. The claim under examination, quoted word for word from an earlier receipt, is that the trained lineage feeds itself and stays going in the harder world on every seed, while an untrained control does not. The second half of that sentence is what makes it a discriminator rather than a success story.

The protocol asks for a spread of world seeds, chosen for a realistic mix rather than for easy food, and frozen into the receipt before the run. For each seed: a fresh world at full pressure, nothing given, no props, and two twins, one carrying frozen memory from the earlier lineage and one starting from nothing. Both run for at least a stated stretch, and a fixed list of things is recorded. Three verdicts are defined in advance, and the falsifier gets its own line: any seed where the trained twin dies and the untrained one keeps going.

Then comes the discipline, which is unusually explicit. Confirmation is to come from an independent channel rather than from the system's own report. The receipt is fenced against particular words. Only one such test may be in flight at a time, and if another is running this one waits. And a closing section states what the test would not settle: it speaks to behaviour under full pressure against a proper control, and to nothing beyond that.
