This is a pre-registration, and it is also a correction. The verdict is withheld.

The motivation says what went wrong before. The second run of this test pooled its arms and could not attribute outcomes to individual bodies, so the question it was meant to answer could not be settled. This third run is designed to remove that confounder rather than to argue around it.

The changes are specific. The two designs run in separate worlds, with different seeds and no shared start, so neither arm can help or hinder the other. Every relevant event is attributed to a named body through an independent query, with a time and a place attached to it. The pass condition is a stated ratio between the two per-body rates, with a middle verdict for an improvement that is real but smaller than that, and a failure if the newer design does no better than the older one.

The closing rule is the part worth noticing. The older, weaker receipt is not deleted and not quietly replaced. It stays on the record as the honest account of what was found, and this run either promotes the claim or withdraws it.
