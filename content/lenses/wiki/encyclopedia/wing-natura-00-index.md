Nature is the subject of this wing; the program is not. Everything gathered here is cited from outside work, so it contributes no evidence to the program's own results and raises no rung of its build. That fence comes before anything else, because the wing would otherwise read as though somebody else's published numbers had settled what the program has yet to show. The program's honest position is untouched by every chapter that follows: a developmental active-inference simulation, a toy world, never a person. Two vocabularies for evidence run side by side and are never merged. One tracks the build's status. The other sorts nature's regularities by how much independent corroboration stands behind each one. Lived experience stays sovereign from measurement too, so a cultural frequency table can be recorded honestly and never converted into physics. The rest is a map: which chapters cover what, which recipes can be built, a reading order, and a frank list of rows the amendments did not fix.

<!--CLEAR-->

The wing exists to answer one mandate: that a builder be able to reason about design down to the cell, about ratios and frequencies, about mind and body and world, and about how to make new science with falsifiable evidence, using nature as the authority on where to look next. That mandate is answerable only if the wing keeps a distinction the rest of the corpus never had to make, which is the difference between a gate the program held and a measurement somebody else published. Both are evidence. They are not the same kind and they do not license the same moves.

The sovereignty rule is stated before anything else. A nature citation is never a gate for the program, and a chapter that lets a literature citation imply a capability has crossed lanes and is defective regardless of how good the citation is. The two ledgers are cross-referenced by explicit audited link only, never by merge, and there is no operation that takes a row from one and a row from the other and produces a stronger row of either kind.

The classes are then summarised in three groups. A measured group says how much independent corroboration exists. A derived group says the assumptions are the fence. A fenced group carries no usable value and says precisely why not, and those ways of being empty are deliberately not interchangeable: an absence in the literature is a fact about the world's knowledge, while an untraced value is a fact about this corpus's own homework.

The page also keeps lived experience sovereign from measurement. A cultural frequency table is inadmissible as physics and may be recorded as an honest signal, never converted into a measured one.

The bulk of the page is two tables mapping the reference chapters and the buildable recipes, each with the fence read from that chapter's own words. A reading order follows, deliberately putting method before content and dimensionless groups before ratios. Finally the page prints what the amendments did not fix: a set of rows still carrying untraced values, and the day on which the chapters and the ledger disagreed in the one column an amendment existed to fix.
