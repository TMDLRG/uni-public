Everything gathered in this wing is borrowed science. Other people did the measuring and published it, and the wing quotes them, so it contributes no evidence to the program's own results. The program's own subject is a developmental simulation — a toy world, and never a person. Both fences are stated before anything else, because otherwise somebody else's published numbers would read as though they had settled what the program has yet to show.

The wing answers one mandate. A builder should be able to reason about design down to the cell, about ratios and frequencies, about mind and body and world, and about how to make new science with falsifiable evidence. Nature is the authority on where to look next. That is answerable only if the wing keeps a distinction the rest of the corpus never had to make: the difference between a test the program itself passed and a measurement somebody else published. Both are evidence. They are not the same kind and they do not license the same moves.

A chapter that lets a literature citation imply a capability has crossed lanes and is defective, however good the citation is. The two ledgers are cross-referenced by explicit audited link only, never by merge, and no operation takes a row from one and a row from the other to produce a stronger row of either kind.

The classes are then summarised in three groups. A measured group says how much independent corroboration exists. A derived group says the assumptions are the fence. A fenced group carries no usable value and says precisely why not. Those ways of being empty are deliberately not interchangeable: an absence in the literature is a fact about the world's knowledge, while an untraced value is a fact about this corpus's own homework.

The page also keeps lived experience sovereign from measurement. A cultural frequency table is inadmissible as physics and may be recorded as an honest signal, never converted into a measured one.

The bulk of the page is two tables mapping the reference chapters and the buildable recipes, each with the fence read from that chapter's own words. A reading order follows, deliberately putting method before content and dimensionless groups before ratios. Finally the page prints what the amendments did not fix: a set of rows still carrying untraced values, and the day on which the chapters and the ledger disagreed in the one column an amendment existed to fix.
