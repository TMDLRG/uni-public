This chapter argues that leading with your own failures is a method, not an achievement. Publishing the negatives, fencing the claims and tracing every figure to a ledger row is a discipline the program can show works. It is never evidence that the underlying science is right. Saying that we are honest about what we have not shown is a true sentence; it is not a substitute for having shown something, and the chapter exists so that nobody hears the second when the first was said. The call to action it mandates is exact: help us get this independently verified, never fund the vision. It also carries its own unfinished business. A relationship-first engagement approach is a targeted bet being instrumented, not a result, and the promised independent third-party check has not yet happened.

<!--CLEAR-->

The chapter's honest position is small and load-bearing: the honesty posture is a method, carded as such, and it can never be raised into a capability claim.

The method has two halves. First, publish the negatives at the front, in a visible box saying what has not been shown, and treat them as the credibility rather than as something to bury. Second, a test-enforced guard on public wording, so that public copy never externalises the internal framework vocabulary and never features an internal channel handle. The mandated call to action follows from both: ask the reader to help verify the work independently, never to fund a vision. Because the method asserts no capability, its falsifier is a violation rather than a measurement, such as public copy carrying the fenced vocabulary, stating an inflated claim, or asking for funding instead of scrutiny.

The chapter then records the one time the method was run end to end. A multi-document press kit was authored with no autonomous server-side language model in the authoring path, and every figure in it traces to a ledger row backed by a one-command reproduction. That run is carded as test-covered, which is not the same as feature-working, and its falsifiers are concrete: a figure that cannot be traced, a reproduction that fails, or a content model discovered in the authoring path. The kit is described as a faithful messenger, not a result.

A third section is where the fence is loudest. The recorded snapshot of ledger rows and negatives must be cited as a snapshot with its provenance attached, because the negative count cannot be rebuilt from the rows on show. So the chapter headlines only the negatives the ledger body actually enumerates, including a reading result that is a count baseline rather than comprehension, a specialist language gain whose overall score worsens, a comprehension wall recorded three times over, and a metabolism organ that helped foraging while building went backwards.

The final section fences the outcome of the posture itself. The engagement ladder it describes is a designed mechanism whose origin was a recorded failure, and its lift is targeted and pending, not shown. Until registered targets move on a held window, the honest copy is that this is a bet being instrumented.
