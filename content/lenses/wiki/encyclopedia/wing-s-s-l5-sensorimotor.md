Mind and body as one loop is the framing of this rung of the developmental ladder, and the chapter carries a result the program is proud of together with the negative sitting right beside it. What sits on that ladder is a developmental active-inference simulation, never a person, so the honest headline is not that the program learned to move. It is narrower and more interesting: under one registered synthetic protocol, an inference-driven controller beat a matched random-action control on one axis and lost on a structurally distinct axis. That symmetry, a pass delimited by its own paired negative, is the result, and citing the pass without the negative fails review. Three limits travel with the positive figure every time it appears. Both designs ran in a synthetic process, so this is neither a live-appliance nor a recorded-hardware result. The passing agent is far from optimal, plateauing well short of an oracle. And the live behavioural tallies are parked and accruing, not a sealed gate.

<!--CLEAR-->

Two structurally distinct embodiment designs were written down in full, bar and all, before either was built, and each was then held once. Both ran inside a simulation — a toy world, not a person. They differ on at least two of the four axes the method requires, so they count as genuinely distinct attempts rather than reruns of one idea.

The first design, on a fast immediate-reward axis, held a positive delta over a policy-shuffle control, and because the verdict is the interval bound rather than the point estimate, the load-bearing fact is that the lower bound stays above zero. The second design was a slow bottleneck with a compressed sensory channel and delayed reward. It held negative, and its entire interval sits below zero, so on that axis the active channel did not merely fail to help: it underperformed the random-action control. That is recorded as a first-class result rather than a buried partial.

A method note travels with the pair. One structurally distinct held negative has accrued, and the program's own discipline requires several before any published bound is owed, so no bound is owed here and none is claimed. A single clean negative is a result, not a bound.

Alongside the synthetic gates the chapter records a live mechanism result with mixed classes, which must be read as mechanism evidence rather than as the capability claim. A proprioceptive prior breaks a non-identifiable factor so the posterior moves off zero, and that is described as the body sensing its own configuration, modelled and never felt. A live gate saw a lineage bootstrap a craft chain confirmed by the game server, and harvest collapses sharply under motor ablation. An offline suite is test-covered, which certifies the code path rather than a runtime capability.

A next design has been accrued and signed at the design level only. It changes all four axes and implements a closed-loop path from policy to setpoint to residual to correction. It is a design to build, not a result: statuses are unchanged, and even a clean pass would be limited, since it would be neither general motor intelligence nor human-like embodiment, and it would not erase the recorded negative.
