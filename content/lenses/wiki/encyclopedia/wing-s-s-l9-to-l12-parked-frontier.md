Four rungs at the top of the ladder have nothing standing behind them, and this is where the encyclopedia says so. They are reasoning and conscience, wisdom, dreaming, and creativity reaching toward measurable awareness: the far end of a developmental active-inference simulation that stays a bounded peek at a toy world and never a person. The single load-bearing fact is that nothing here is claimed. Two of those rungs are parked and two are not yet built, and all four are recorded as claimed-but-unearned, which is itself a standing limit rather than a soft placeholder. Two rungs now carry a signed gate design, and the top rung carries a signed public framing paragraph and a ten-point checklist any future work must pass before such a gate may even be proposed. Read those exactly as what they are: gates to build and guardrails on a question, not results. A signed design raises nothing.

<!--CLEAR-->

Four rungs of the ladder sit above the line the program has earned, and each is recorded at its honest emptiness. The ladder they sit on belongs to a simulation — a toy world, not a person.

The reasoning and conscience rung is parked. The levels exist in the ladder design but are not separately gated or passed: there is no evaluation on data set aside for it, no interval verdict and no signature confirming a reasoning capability. The level becomes testable only once a gate is registered in advance, sealed, signed, defined and run. That has not happened, and a sign-off to park is owed. Two design artifacts point toward the region and must never be inflated into capability, a designed roadmap that is explicitly not built and a proposed first gate that is signed but not run. Even a future pass on that gate would be limited, and would not unpark that level generally. The travelling negative sits one step down the ladder, where several structurally distinct designs all tuned their own terms off and left a residue whose interval spans zero.

The wisdom rung is the shortest entry, for the honest reason that there is the least to say: no engine, no run, no gate, and a sign-off owed. A governance note travels with it. The single ledger, a list rows are added to and never edited, must stay singular, because a second writable ledger would let an aspirational level be written up as progress somewhere the constitution cannot calibrate it down.

The dreaming rung is not yet built, and it is limited hard. Its field for the result that would show it wrong is the most honest in the chapter: there is nothing built to falsify, and the status is the absence of any artifact. A proposed gate is recorded as designed and not built, and the vocabulary limit is load-bearing, since offline replay is a design primitive and never dreaming.

The top rung carries the hardest limit. The owner's stated aim is recorded, and immediately recorded as never claimed. The level is posed as an open, falsifiable question rather than an answer, and the only legitimate movement is a scoped published negative, never a capability assertion. A signed paragraph, installed word for word, states that today the answer is not known, not claimed and not implied, and spells out exactly what passing a future proxy suite would and would not license. A ten-condition entry checklist then fixes what any such proposal must carry before it may even be put forward.
